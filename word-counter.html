Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

Simply paste or type your text in the input box. The tool counts words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in real-time as you type.

Is the word count accurate?

Yes, the word counter uses standard word boundary detection to accurately count words separated by spaces, tabs, and line breaks.

Can I check the reading time of my text?

Yes, the tool estimates reading time based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.
