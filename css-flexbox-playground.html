Frequently Asked Questions

            

                
                

                    
How do I align items vertically in Flexbox?

                    
Use `align-items: center` for single-line flex containers, or `align-content: center` for multi-line wrapped containers.

                

                
                

                    
What does flex-grow do?

                    
It defines the ability for a flex item to grow if necessary, specifying how much remaining container space it should take up.

                

                
                

                    
Can I copy the generated CSS?

                    
Yes, click 'Copy CSS' to copy the container and item rules directly to your clipboard.
