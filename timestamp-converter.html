Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Unix timestamp?

The number of seconds that have elapsed since January 1, 1970 UTC (the Unix Epoch).

Does it support milliseconds?

Yes, handles both 10-digit (seconds) and 13-digit (milliseconds) timestamps.

Does it show local and UTC time?

Yes, both your local timezone and UTC timestamps are displayed side-by-side.
