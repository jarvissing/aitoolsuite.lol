Frequently Asked Questions

What does CIDR notation mean?

Classless Inter-Domain Routing specifies the prefix length (bits in the network mask).

How many usable hosts are in a /24 subnet?

A /24 subnet provides 256 total IP addresses and 254 usable host addresses.
