Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

At AI Tool Suite (accessible from https://aitoolsuite.lol), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by AI Tool Suite and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Processing Guarantee

Our core philosophy is zero-server data exposure. All calculations, formatters, generators, conversions, and encoding utilities (including JSON, CSV, Base64, UUID, AI Token counting, and cURL parsers) execute 100% locally in your web browser using JavaScript and the Web Crypto API.

Your sensitive code, API keys, tokens, SQL queries, and text inputs are never transmitted, stored, logged, or processed on any remote server.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, AI Tool Suite may use standard cookies to maintain user preferences and optimize user experience. We use lightweight, privacy-focused analytics to monitor aggregate visitor counts without collecting personally identifiable information (PII).

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Advertising Partners

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to aitoolsuite.lol and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on AI Tool Suite. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

4. Third Party Privacy Policies

AI Tool Suite's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights & GDPR Data Protection Rights

Under CCPA and GDPR, every user is entitled to data transparency. Because AI Tool Suite does not collect personal data, account registrations, or server-side logs, no personal profiles or tracking identifiers are retained or sold.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, visit our Contact Page or reach us directly at agencymeta.app@gmail.com.
