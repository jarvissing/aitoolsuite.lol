Frequently Asked Questions

Which DNS records are checked?

A, AAAA (IPv6), CNAME, MX (Mail), and TXT verification records.

How is the lookup performed?

Queries are sent securely to Cloudflare DNS over HTTPS (DoH) in real-time.

Does it show TTL values?

Yes, TTL (Time to Live) and raw target addresses are displayed for each record.
