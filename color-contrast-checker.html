Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum WCAG contrast ratio?

WCAG AA requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (18pt+).

How is relative luminance calculated?

Using the official W3C formula based on sRGB color space conversion.
