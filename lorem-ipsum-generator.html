Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy text used in the printing and typesetting industry since the 1500s. It serves as placeholder text to demonstrate the visual layout of a document without meaningful content.

Why use Lorem Ipsum instead of real text?

Using placeholder text helps designers and developers focus on visual elements like typography, spacing, and layout without being distracted by readable content.

Is Lorem Ipsum real Latin?

Lorem Ipsum is derived from a work by Cicero written in 45 BC, but the text has been altered and scrambled over the centuries so it is not proper Latin.
